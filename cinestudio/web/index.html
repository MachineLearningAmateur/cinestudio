New template the mannequin copies the motion of a dance video (a Blender shot)

SAM 3D Body reads the dancer's 3D motion (one GPU job, about 2 minutes for 10 s), then Blender keys the mannequin with it: a still front camera and a plain floor. Choose a background (a backplate or a 360° panorama) in the Video tab.

A .blend needs a mannequin (collection Mannequin with the rig MannequinRig) and a camera: your character replaces the mannequin. A video needs one person (a stand-in) in an empty place: your character replaces that person. Sounds in the .blend are kept.

Layers

A template has three layers (Blender collections): the characters' mannequins, Props (objects that move or that a character touches) and Set (the static place). Their words go into the video text ("In the scene: ...") and into a backplate drawn over the set.

Props one line per prop, or a pattern such as Ball_* for several: the same concrete words everywhere

    Template check

    The template rules (shot check): read from the .blend by Blender in the background (no render, no GPU job). Errors break a video; warnings often mislead it; fix them in Blender and check again.
